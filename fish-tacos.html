Fish Tacos

Crisp, lightly spiced fish tacos with crunchy cabbage, lime crema, and warm tortillas.

Prep: 20 minutes Cook: 10 minutes Serves: 4

Ingredients

  • 1 pound white fish fillets, such as cod, tilapia, or mahi-mahi
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 8 small corn or flour tortillas
  • 2 cups shredded cabbage
  • 1/4 cup chopped cilantro
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges

Lime Crema

  • 1/2 cup sour cream or Greek yogurt
  • 1 tablespoon lime juice
  • 1 teaspoon honey
  • Pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. Stir together the sour cream, lime juice, honey, and salt. Set the crema aside.
  2. Pat the fish dry, then rub it with olive oil, chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, garlic powder, and salt.
  3. Heat a skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the fish for 3 to 4 minutes per side, until it flakes easily with a fork.
  4. Warm the tortillas in a dry skillet or microwave.
  5. Break the fish into large pieces and divide it among the tortillas.
  6. Top with cabbage, cilantro, avocado, lime crema, and a squeeze of fresh lime.

Tip

For extra crunch, toss the cabbage with a little lime juice and salt before serving.
